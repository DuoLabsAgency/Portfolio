01 — About

What We Do

Duo Labs is a performance marketing team working with e-commerce and lead-generation brands across Egypt, the UAE, and Kuwait.

We don't just run ads. Most accounts we take over don't have a media-buying problem — they have a systems problem: no structured testing, budget concentrated on two hero products, tracking that doesn't reflect real revenue, or a funnel with no top-of-funnel at all. We fix the system first, then scale it.

That means creative direction and technical discipline in the same engagement — the hook that stops the scroll, and the pixel, testing framework, and budget logic underneath it that turn that moment into a measurable result.

Full-Funnel Strategy
Campaign architecture built per objective — winners, testing, and retargeting stop competing for the same budget.
Testing Systems
Full-catalogue testing frameworks that spread risk across the whole product range, not 2–3 hero SKUs.
Tracking Infrastructure
Pixel and CAPI setup, plus offline-to-online sales tracking for businesses with no digital checkout.
Scaling & Budget Logic
Push & punishment rules, vertical and horizontal scaling in parallel, ROAS defended through the scale-up.
CRO & AOV
Bundle strategy, pricing, and on-site conversion fixes — because scaling into a leaky funnel only inflates CPA.
Creative Direction
UGC/AGC briefing, format testing, and angle development driven by live ROAS data rather than guesswork.
